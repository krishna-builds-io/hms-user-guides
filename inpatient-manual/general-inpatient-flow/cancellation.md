## 5.1 Cancel a Scheduled Admission (before Admit)

**Navigate to:** Healthcare → Inpatient Record → [Admission Scheduled] → Cancel Admission

**Steps:**

1. Open the record before the patient is given a bed.
2. Click **Cancel Admission** and confirm.

**Expected result:** The admission is cancelled. No bed is used and no charges are added.
