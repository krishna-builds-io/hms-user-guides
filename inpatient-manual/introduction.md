This manual shows how to use the **Inpatient (IP)** module of the Hospital Management System (HMS).

- **I. General Inpatient Flow** follows one patient, ARJUN KUMAR, through the full journey: registering, admitting, adding charges during the stay, and discharging with the bill paid.
- **II. Ward Workflows** explains each ward workflow on its own (Lab Test, Radiology, Clinical Procedure, Discharge Summary, Pharmacy, Surgery).
- **III–VI** cover Corporate and Insurance billing, IP refunds and bulk payments.

## How to read this manual

Every task is written the same way:

- **Navigate to** tells you where to go.
- **Steps** tells you what to do.
- A screenshot shows the screen.
- **Expected result** tells you what you should see.

Fields marked with a red star (*) must be filled.

**Flow:** Register patient → Admit to a bed → Add charges during the stay → Bill the tests → Close bill & take payment → Discharge → Print the bill
